# CampusAttend OS - Institutional Director Guide

## 1. Executive Dashboard & Institutional Pulse
The **Director Dashboard** provides real-time, college-wide visibility without data lag:

- **Active Sessions Counter:** Total lectures actively being delivered across campus right now.
- **Campus-Wide Headcount:** Total students currently verified in classrooms vs total enrolled.
- **Departmental Activity Matrix:** Live list of all academic departments, active lecture halls, and conducting faculty.
- **Global Search:** Search instantly for any student (by Roll Number, Name, or Registration Number) or faculty member across the entire university.

---

## 2. Non-Evaluative Department Comparison
CampusAttend OS implements objective statistical comparison across academic departments without subjective ranking:

- **Metrics Displayed:**
  - Total degree programs and cohort sections.
  - Active faculty count.
  - Average attendance percentage across all conducted lectures.
  - Total sessions conducted year-to-date.
- **Strict Anti-Ranking Principle:** The platform strictly prohibits arbitrary "best-to-worst" evaluative ranking or competitive leaderboards. Data is sorted alphabetically by department code to uphold institutional collegiality while providing transparent administrative insight.

---

## 3. Session Report Oversight & Administrative Approvals
1. Navigate to **Session Reports**.
2. Filter by Department, Program, Academic Year, Date Range, or Status (`Pending`, `Approved`, `Flagged`).
3. Click any report to view:
   - Full student attendance roster with verification methods (`dynamic_qr`, `manual_faculty`, `admin_override`).
   - Smart display hardware pairing logs and IP addresses.
   - Faculty lecture notes and submission timestamps.
4. **Approve / Correct:**
   - Click **"Approve Report"** to formally lock the record into the institution's official archive.
   - If discrepancies are noted, enter administrative review remarks and adjust records with full audit trail attribution.

---

## 4. University Timetable Management & Real-Time Sync
The Director possesses institution-wide authority to manage master timetables and handle operational disruptions:

### Emergency Rescheduling Workflow:
1. Open **Master Timetable** > Select the target Section and Day.
2. Select the specific lecture slot.
3. Modify the classroom (e.g., relocate from `LH-101` to `Turing Hall North`) or adjust timings.
4. Click **"Save Changes & Broadcast"**.
5. **Zero-Latency Propagation:**
   - The student web portal and mobile apps receive the updated room assignment instantly.
   - The faculty schedule reflects the new room.
   - The smart displays in both the old room and new room re-route their session feeds automatically.
